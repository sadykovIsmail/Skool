## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Do I need to install anything?
**A:** Nope! Everything runs in your browser. It's just HTML/CSS/JavaScript.

### Q: Will my posts save?
**A:** Yes! Posts and upvotes save in your browser's local storage. They persist even after closing and reopening.

### Q: Can I share this with others?
**A:** Absolutely! Share the entire folder or just `app.html`. Others can open it in their browser immediately.

### Q: What about videos?
**A:** Videos stream from Mux/Vimeo (where they're hosted). Your browser pulls them from there. Requires internet.

### Q: Is this safe?
**A:** Completely safe! It's a local app with no servers, no trackers, no accounts. All data stays on your computer.

### Q: Can I change the design?
**A:** Yes! Open `app.html` in any text editor to modify colors, fonts, layout, etc.

### Q: What if I break something?
**A:** Just restore the original `app.html`. Everything is reversible.

### Q: Can I add more features?
**A:** Yes! The code is well-commented. You can add comments, user profiles, direct messaging, etc.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### App won't open?
→ Try `run.bat` first, then try double-clicking `app.html`

### Videos don't play?
→ Check internet connection, refresh page, try different browser

### Posts won't save?
→ Make sure you're not in private/incognito mode

### Colors look weird?
→ Try zooming in/out with Ctrl+/Ctrl- or F11 for fullscreen

### Need more help?
→ Check QUICK_START.md and README.md files
